What it does.

Write it, find it, keep it. Three promises, and nothing else.

spec / 01-03

Capture, don't file

Open a note and start typing. Titles, folders and tags are optional, not a ritual.

Sync is a choice

Notes live in a folder you own. Put it in iCloud, Dropbox or git, or nowhere at all.

Plain Markdown

Every note is a .md file. Your editor of choice stays your editor of choice.

Things you might ask.

Something else? Open an issue on GitHub.

Where do my notes live?+

In a folder on your disk, as plain .md files. The app is a viewer and an editor, not a vault.

Do you collect any data?+

No account and no telemetry. The app never phones home, and the website is a static page.

How do updates work?+

Updates are manual and signed: the app points you at the latest release and you decide when to install.

What platforms are supported?+

macOS 13+, Windows 10+ and modern Linux, on both arm64 and x86_64.
