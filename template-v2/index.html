Things you might ask.

Something else? Open an issue on GitHub.

Is it really free?+

Say the license, then say what it costs to run. Honesty here buys trust everywhere else.

Do you collect any data?+

If the answer is "no", make it specific: what never leaves the machine, and what (if anything) the website measures.

How do updates work?+

Describe the update path in one sentence. If the app checks for updates, say how often and where it checks.

What platforms are supported?+

List the platforms and the minimum versions. Update this answer the day a platform changes, not later.
